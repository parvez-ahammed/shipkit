# Claims and facts

check.mjs fails until every spoken line and every on-screen text of 4 or more words is covered by a row here
(the claim cell must contain the text) and the row names its source. Source = a file and line in the product
(landing page, docs, code), or "example data" for invented names and numbers in mock screens.
The rows below cover the template's own text so a fresh project passes check.mjs. Replace every
"template placeholder" row with your real claim and its source; never ship a placeholder row.
